Executive Summary: Emergency medical and organ transfer vehicles face severe transit delays in Indian metropolitan corridors due to queued traffic at red lights. Traditional GPS preemption either actuates too late or induces extensive secondary gridlock. This project delivers an integrated cyber-physical solution: (1) JeevanPath, an operational cloud dispatch platform, and (2) CC-HLP, a chance-constrained 3D digital twin backed by Eclipse SUMO TraCI microscopic simulation, executing a patented dual-action queue clearance law with hardware-in-the-loop fail-safe interlocks.

Claim 1 • Queue Partitioning
Dual-Action Seam Creation
Partitions the standing queue at red signals into forward-clearing ($N_{\text{crit}}$) and lateral-yielding sets, preventing ambulance blockage without clearing unnecessary vehicles.
Claim 2 • Chance Constraints
Stochastic Green Transitions
Enforces $P(T_{\text{trans}} + T_{\text{clear}} \le \text{ETA}) \ge 1 - \epsilon$ under non-deterministic driver human reaction delay lognormal parameters ($\mu=1.705\,\text{s}$).
Claim 3 • Fail-Safe Cabinet
