Claim 2 • Chance Constraints
Bounded Risk Optimization
Solves $P(T_{\text{trans}} + T_{\text{clear}} \le \text{ETA}_{\text{EV}}) \ge 1 - \epsilon$. Guarantees zero emergency vehicle braking episodes while minimizing non-emergency side-street delay.
